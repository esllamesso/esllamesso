### About Me

I work across two fields that usually live in separate teams: **mobile development** and **AI-powered marketing**.

On the development side, I build Flutter apps backed by Firebase, Supabase, REST APIs and local storage, with Bloc/Cubit for state management. On the marketing side, I currently work as an AI & Digital Marketing Specialist at **MGH Consultancy**, where I use AI workflows to promote educational and professional programs — writing video concepts, scripts and prompts, and producing marketing videos and visual designs.

Previously, I taught Flutter as an instructor at **AMIT Learning** and mentored students as a Technical Mentor in **DEPI Round 3** — reviewing their code, pointing out architectural issues and helping them debug real projects.

Based in Giza, Egypt.
